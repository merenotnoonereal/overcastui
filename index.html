OvercastUI

An in-game web browser for Minecraft — browse the web, read a wiki, or watch video in picture-in-picture without alt-tabbing or pausing your game.

The web, right there on top of Minecraft. Steam-overlay style, and it never pauses your game.

Download on CurseForge Source on GitHub

OvercastUI is a 100% client-side Fabric mod that embeds a full Chromium web browser into Minecraft as a Steam-overlay-style overlay. Summon it with a hotkey (default Shift+Tab) and work with multiple draggable, resizable, tabbed windows on top of your game — read a wiki, look something up, or keep a video playing in Picture-in-Picture, all without alt-tabbing and without ever pausing gameplay.

Features

  • Full web browser in-game — back/forward/reload, address bar, and find-in-page.
  • Multiple draggable, resizable windows with tab support.
  • Picture-in-Picture — keep a video playing in the corner while you play.
  • Split-view — read two pages side by side.
  • Customizable theme — adjust opacity and backdrop.
  • Rebindable hotkey — default Shift+Tab.
  • Tab suspension — idle tabs are suspended to save resources.
  • Never pauses your game.

Great for

  • Reading the Hypixel Skyblock wiki (or any wiki) without leaving the game
  • Watching a video or stream in picture-in-picture while you grind
  • Quick lookups, guides, and reference — mid-game, no interruption

How to use

  1. Install Fabric Loader and Fabric API.
  2. Put the OvercastUI .jar in your mods folder and launch.
  3. Press Shift+Tab (rebindable) in-game to open the overlay.
  4. Open tabs and windows, drag/resize them, and enable Picture-in-Picture or split-view as needed.

FAQ

Is there a mod to browse the web inside Minecraft?
Yes — OvercastUI embeds a full web browser into Minecraft as a Steam-overlay-style overlay. Open it with a hotkey (default Shift+Tab) and browse in draggable, tabbed windows without leaving the game.
Does it pause my game when the browser is open?
No. Unlike alt-tabbing, OvercastUI never pauses your game — your session keeps running underneath.
Can I watch a video or YouTube while playing?
Yes — Picture-in-Picture mode keeps a video playing in the corner while you play.
How do I open the browser overlay?
Press the hotkey, which defaults to Shift+Tab. It's rebindable in the controls settings.
Is it client-side?
Yes — fully client-side; nothing needs to be installed on the server.
